Quick GIGS — Business Plan + GTM

The fastest, most transparent way for Indian businesses to get creative work made.

Vinayak Tower, Vibhuti Khand, Lucknow 226028

Opportunity

  • Businesses need weekly creative work but hiring is slow and fragmented.
  • Quick GIGS turns a rough request into a structured brief, verified match, escrow-protected order and accountable delivery.
  • Initial beachhead: D2C brands, agencies, regional SMBs and creator-led businesses.

Business model

  • Starter, Pro and Studio one-off packages.
  • Monthly team plans with allowance, SLA and consolidated billing.
  • Managed specialist pods and white-label agency delivery.
  • Creators pay zero platform fee and receive 100% of the service subtotal.
  • Customer breakdown shows service amount plus GST only.

12-month GTM

  1. Instrument CRM, funnel events, QA and creator verification.
  2. Run a concierge pilot with founder-led matching.
  3. Publish delivered-work proof and customer case studies.
  4. Grow through referrals, partnerships and SEO/AEO.
  5. Scale only validated paid channels, monthly pods and agency accounts.

90-day targets

  • 30 paid orders
  • At least 80% on-time delivery
  • 10 repeat-order opportunities
  • 5 case studies
  • 30–40 active specialists recruited

Key KPIs

  • Median match time under 15 minutes
  • At least 90% on-time delivery
  • At least 4.6/5 buyer rating
  • 35% second-order rate within 90 days by month 12
  • CAC payback within three months before paid scale
